# Beginner Python Conditional Statements Exercises

**Rules:**

* Use only `if`, `elif`, `else`, `input()`, comparison operators, logical operators, and `random` where required.
* Some exercises require importing the `random` module.
* Do **not** use loops or functions unless instructed.
* No hints or solutions are provided.

---

## Exercise 1 – Even or Odd
